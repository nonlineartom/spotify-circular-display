Link the Spotify account currently playing on Pi Display. Its playlists and saved albums will return automatically whenever that household member takes control.

Link or reauthorize with Spotify

This one-use link expires quickly and is bound to the account currently playing. The household link itself stays saved until it is disconnected or Spotify asks that member to reauthorize. Other, unlinked listeners see House picks — never another person’s library.
